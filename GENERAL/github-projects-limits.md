GitHub Project boards do have a limitation on the number of items they can hold, but the limit was recently increased significantly.

- **Current Limit:** A soft limit of  ** 50,000 items **  per project.
- **Previous Limit:** Older projects used to be strictly limited to  ** 1,200 active items **  (and 10,000 archived items).

Because the 50,000 cap is considered a "soft limit," GitHub has stated that they can expand it even further on a case-by-case basis for large enterprise teams with long-running backlogs.
